🛠️ Troubleshooting Tips

Common Issues & Solutions

  • Nothing happens when I click download? Check your internet connection and try a different browser.
  • File won't extract? Make sure you have WinRAR or 7-Zip installed. Windows includes its own extractor, so right-click and choose "Extract All".
  • Page appears blank? Refresh the page or restart your browser. Clear your cache if needed.
  • Models take too long to generate? Close other programs that use lots of memory. A stable connection helps too.
  • Need more help? Visit our GitHub page and open an issue, or check the community discussions section.
